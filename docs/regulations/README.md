# Stored copies of the regulations

These files are copies of the Official Journal text of the two regulations and of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, which amends the AI Act, from which `api/src/obligations_agent/gates.yaml` quotes the units that the result of an intake cites (MEMO-8 in [docs/spec.md](../spec.md)). The tests in `api/tests/test_gates.py` find every quote of `gates.yaml` word for word in the copy of its document, after the header that ends with the line `---`, and check in the copy that the text directive of each link marks exactly that text. That a browser does the same on EUR-Lex was checked by opening each kind of link in Chromium. A quote can be checked after the web page changes.

The repository's MIT license does not cover these files. They are the text of the European Union, used under its terms of reuse:

| File | Document | Terms of reuse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai-act.txt` | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), CELEX 32024R1689, as published in the Official Journal | © European Union; reuse authorised under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html) |
| `gdpr.txt` | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation), CELEX 32016R0679, as published in the Official Journal | © European Union; reuse authorised under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html) |
| `ai-act-2026-1744.txt` | Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), CELEX 32026R1744, as published in the Official Journal | © European Union; reuse authorised under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html) |

Each copy is the XHTML of the Publications Office (Cellar), taken with `curl -sS -L -H "Accept: application/xhtml+xml" -H "Accept-Language: eng" https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>`, as text from `textutil -convert txt`. Its header names the Cellar document and its version. The text keeps the non-breaking spaces of the Official Journal, such as the one in "Annex III"; the quotes in `gates.yaml` use plain spaces, and the tests count any run of whitespace as one space.

A quote links to the Official Journal text that the copies hold. A unit that the result only links, such as an assumption, an item that is not checked yet or AI Act Article 113 for the dates of the rows, opens the AI Act as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in the consolidated text of 27 July 2026 (CELEX 02024R1689-20260727), so that the user checks it against the text in force. That regulation amends none of the units that `gates.yaml` quotes from the AI Act's own text, which read the same in the consolidated text. It replaced two units that the rows need, the new AI Act Article 4(1) and Article 111(2), so these are quoted from the copy of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and link to its Official Journal text. No act amends the GDPR, so its links open the Official Journal text.
